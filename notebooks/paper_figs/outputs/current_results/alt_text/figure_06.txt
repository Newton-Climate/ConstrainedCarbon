Figure 6 summarizes warming-vulnerability constraints at a 100-year horizon. Panel A shows posterior carbon-loss medians with 95 percent credible intervals by ecosystem and observation subset. Panel B shows the slow-mode fraction of excess respiration with the same subset ordering. Panel C compares the uncertainty reduction from adding respired radiocarbon or soil radiocarbon relative to the stocks_only baseline subset. Points above the one-to-one line indicate greater value from soil radiocarbon, while points below indicate greater value from respired radiocarbon. Marker color encodes the combined value from using all observation types together, and each label reports the combined value minus the better single radiocarbon type, i.e. the marginal gain from combining.
