Figure 3 summarizes posterior turnover structure and effective model complexity for the all_observations observation subset. Panel A shows median, 50 percent, and 95 percent credible intervals for turnover time on a logarithmic axis by ecosystem and turnover mode. Panel B shows the effective number of resolved modes, defined here as the sum of averaging-kernel diagonal values across the mode set. Panel C either shows topology-comparison support when supplied or explicitly reports that topology support is unavailable rather than inventing values. The principal conclusion is that available observations resolve only a limited number of effective turnover modes and the resolved complexity differs among ecosystems.
