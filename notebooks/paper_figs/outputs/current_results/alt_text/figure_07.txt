Figure 7 is an illustrative CESM comparison. Panel A compares turnover-time spectra between the observation-constrained model and CESM, keeping the two sources visually distinct. Panel B compares present-day total stocks and baseline respiration on one-to-one axes. Panel C compares warming-response magnitudes, and Panel D compares the fraction of excess respiration arising from slow or old carbon. The principal conclusion is that similar present-day stocks or fluxes do not guarantee similar turnover structure or the same attribution of future carbon release.
