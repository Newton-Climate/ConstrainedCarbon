Presentation-related files in `notebooks/`
=========================================

This manifest lists the presentation assets and the scripts that generate them.

Scripts
-------
- `build_ecosystem_complexity_presentation.js`
  Builds the final slide deck from the figure assets below.
- `qa_ecosystem_complexity_presentation.py`
  Runs a basic PPTX layout QA pass for overflow and off-slide elements.
- `headline_figures.py`
  Generates the canonical cross-site summary figures used in the deck.
- `cross_site_information.py`
  Generates the cross-site DFS-by-observation-type comparison figure.
- `atm_14c_timeseries.py`
  Generates the atmospheric radiocarbon sampling-window figure.
- `soil_14c_by_biome.py`
  Generates the biome-distribution soil radiocarbon figure.
- `invert_hf_sierra.py`
  Generates the Harvard Forest radiocarbon inversion summary figure.
- `harvard_optimal_model.py`
  Generates the Harvard Forest 8-panel three-pool OE summary figure.
- `barrow_model.py`
  Generates the Barrow 8-panel canonical three-pool OE summary figure.
- `averaging_kernel_figure.py`
  Generates the side-by-side τ-block averaging-kernel comparison for the two sites.
- `barrow_model_israd.py`
  Compatibility wrapper that writes the same canonical Barrow figure under an alias filename.

Figures used in the slide deck
------------------------------
- `atmospheric_delta14c_sampling_windows.png`
  Atmospheric Δ14C history with Harvard Forest and Barrow sampling windows.
- `soil_delta14c_biome_distribution_with_sites.png`
  Biome-level soil Δ14C distributions, annotated with Harvard Forest and Barrow.
- `harvard_forest_radiocarbon_inversion_summary.png`
  Harvard Forest radiocarbon inversion figure used in the site-results section.
- `harvard_forest_three_pool_oe_summary.png`
  Harvard Forest 8-panel three-pool optimal-estimation summary.
- `barrow_canonical_information_content_analysis.png`
  Barrow canonical information-content figure used for the Barrow panel in the deck.
- `canonical_respired_delta14c_model_observation_fits.png`
  Cross-site posterior respired Δ14C model-vs-observation fit figure.
- `canonical_posterior_turnover_time_comparison.png`
  Cross-site posterior turnover-time comparison figure.
- `harvard_forest_canonical_information_content_analysis.png`
  Harvard Forest canonical information-content figure.
- `averaging_kernel_tau_comparison.png`
  Side-by-side turnover-time averaging kernels for Harvard Forest and Barrow.
- `cross_site_observation_type_dfs_comparison.png`
  Cross-site DFS comparison by observation type.
- `model_complexity_information_saturation.png`
  DFS-vs-model-complexity ladder showing information saturation near 3 pools.
- `clm/clm_emulator_posterior.png`
  CLM-emulated posterior τ comparison against the radiocarbon-constrained posterior.
- `clm/clm_emulator_14c_mismatch.png`
  CLM-emulated respired Δ14C mismatch against observations.
